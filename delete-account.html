Delete Your MileMatex Account

Option 1: Delete it yourself in the app (fastest)

If you still have MileMatex installed:

  1. Open the app and log in.
  2. Go to Account → Delete Account.
  3. Enter your password and confirm.

This immediately and permanently deletes your account.

Option 2: Request deletion without the app

If you no longer have the app installed, email us at milematexx@outlook.com from the email address associated with your MileMatex account, with the subject line "Account Deletion Request." We will delete your account and associated data within 30 days of receiving your request.

What gets deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes:

  • Your account information (email, password, preferences).
  • Your routes, delivery stops, and route history.
  • Your active and past device sessions.

Some data may be retained for a limited period where we are required to by law (for example, financial or audit records), consistent with our Privacy Policy.

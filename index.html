MileMatex Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 8, 2026

MileMatex ("we," "us," or "our") provides a route-planning application for delivery drivers. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect your information.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information

We collect your email address and password when you create an account. This information is used to create, maintain, and secure your account.

Location Data

We collect your precise location, including location data in the background when the app is not actively open. This information is used to:

  • Plan and optimize delivery routes.
  • Automatically detect when you arrive at a delivery stop.
  • Provide other location-based features necessary for the app's core functionality.

Camera and Photos

MileMatex may access your device's camera or photos to scan shipping labels and extract delivery addresses.

Scanned images are processed for this purpose and are not permanently stored by MileMatex.

Route and Stop Data

We collect and process information you create or provide within the app, including:

  • Delivery addresses.
  • Delivery stop information.
  • Delivery status.
  • Route information and route history.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect solely to provide and improve the app's core functionality, including:

  • Planning and optimizing delivery routes.
  • Detecting arrivals at delivery stops.
  • Managing delivery stops and their status.
  • Providing mapping and address-related features.
  • Maintaining and securing your account.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Data Sharing

MileMatex uses Google Maps Platform to provide mapping, routing, and address lookup services.

To provide these features, certain location and address information may be shared with Google as necessary. Your use of these services may also be subject to Google's privacy policies and terms.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

You can permanently delete your MileMatex account and associated data at any time from within the app.

To delete your account:

Account → Delete Account

When you delete your account, your account information, routes, and stop data are permanently removed from our systems, subject to any data that we may be required to retain by law.

5. Security

We use industry-standard security measures, including encryption for data in transit and secure authentication practices, to help protect your account and information.

However, no method of transmitting or storing information electronically can be guaranteed to be completely secure.
